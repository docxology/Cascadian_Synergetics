# Design notes

## A world without right angles

The grove keeps one rule visibly: nothing grows square. Trunks taper and lean, the UI is circles, soft blobs and 120-degree hex chips, the hats have three-sided crowns, and the edge of the screen dissolves into mist. The incursions are the exception. They are the only squares in the world, and the patrol's work is to fold them away.

## Show, don't tell

The first versions explained themselves with a briefing, pop-ups that taught the geometry, and a closing card that recapped the run. All of that went. What remains:

- The first incursion hums inside the opening view, laying its grid over the moss.
- Places are named on routed trail signs and in a small chip at the top.
- The action button names the action, not the idea.
- The rangers talk about boots, coffee, elk and slugs. The radio carries deadpan bulletins.
- The finale has no card: the view lifts into the canopy and settles back.
- Unfinished work pulls the lantern toward it; in the storybook it also glows at the screen's edge in its own colour.

## Palette and type

Fog sage, redwood bark, canopy green, quadray brass, lantern amber, and the scarlet of the gallery's tensegrity poster. Fraunces (with its soft, wonky axes) for display, Alegreya Sans for reading, Special Elite for the Office's typed paperwork. The notebook is green ledger paper.

## Scenes from the gallery

The Office of Synergetics Services gallery supplied scenes, not just objects:

- the station at redwood dusk (the geodesic HQ)
- salmon around a luminous tetrahedron (the creek pool)
- ants converging under a rural post-office lamp
- cuboctahedral ash over the station (storybook)
- clay archivists and a file-eating tetrahedron (the storybook's mailbox, and a radio bulletin)
- a red sun over interlocking peaks (storybook, Lookout Rise)
- moonlit tensegrity roots in a flooded forest (the ghost forest's mast)
- the fire lookout, the pins, coins, patches and ferry token
